# fiordland-rail

Static site, no build step. Plain ES modules plus JSON data. Leaflet comes from cdnjs, and the map tiles are OpenStreetMap.

- Run `npm test` after any change to `js/plan.js` or `data/`.
- Keep personal details (home address, phone numbers, email addresses) out of this repo.
- Planning notes live in Andrew's Obsidian vault: Watkins Family/Projects/Holiday 2027 (Vision, Constraints and Flexibility, Research - Cruise and Rail). Keep the site's data consistent with them.
- Card costs are for two people in NZD. Mark unverified figures with `st: "est"`.
