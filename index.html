1 · Timeline

When do the trains and the boats line up?

Rail, ferry and Fiordland cruise availability from October 2026 to June 2028, alongside tourist peaks and sandfly season. Solid bars are published dates. Hatched bars are projected from past patterns or not yet confirmed.

No sandfly-free period exists

Sandflies are in Fiordland all year. They peak October to April and are lowest in July and August. Spring and autumn fail the agreed constraint.

Winter: fewest sandflies, no Coastal Pacific

The Picton–Christchurch train doesn't run May–August. The RealNZ Navigator sails in May and August, with no aircraft needed.

September is the middle ground

Outside the sandfly peak. The Coastal Pacific start date is unconfirmed, and the only cruise found so far needs a helicopter.

    Not costed yet
    • Timing with Penny and Charlie. They have been offered the house while we're away and would look after the plants and hens. Agree when they arrive and leave; they may not need to be there the whole time.
    • Sandflies. October 2027 is chosen for the Wanderer, inside the October–April peak. This misses the agreed sandfly constraint: check with Millie. Take repellent and cover up.
    • Where the car stays while we're away. Options: long-stay parking at Half Moon Bay, on site at Brookby (ask them), or a friend's driveway.
    • Rental car pick-up and return. No rental desk at Christchurch station is confirmed. Apex has a city branch at 150 Kilmore Street (8:00am–4:59pm daily, no after-hours pick-up). Collect the morning after the train arrives; drop off by 5pm the day before the 07:00 train north. A short taxi from the station is fine.
    • Night counts are approximate. An extra Wellington or Picton night, or a Fiordland weather buffer day, adds to both the total and the kennel bill.
    • Kennel cough booster must be current for the whole stay. Check the vaccination booklets.
    • Travel insurance that covers weather cancellations in Fiordland.
