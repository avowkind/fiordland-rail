    4 · Things to think about

    Not costed yet
    • Penny and Charlie house-sit from Wednesday 29 September (confirmed). Agree the handover for the plants and hens, and when they leave after we're back on Monday 11 October.
    • Sandflies. October is inside the October–April peak. This misses the agreed sandfly constraint: check with Millie. Take repellent and cover up.
    • No car of our own. We take the foot ferry, and Finn or a one-day rental gets the dogs to Brookby and back, so there's no parking for two weeks.
    • Rental car in Christchurch. No rental desk at the station is confirmed. Apex has a city branch at 150 Kilmore Street (8:00am–4:59pm daily, no after-hours pick-up). Collect Saturday 2 October; return Friday 8 October before 5pm, ready for the morning train north. A short taxi from the station is fine.
    • No spare day before the cruise. Monday 4 October's drive to Te Anau has no buffer if the road closes. Leave early, and have travel insurance that covers weather cancellations in Fiordland.
    • Kennel cough booster must be current for the whole stay. Check the vaccination booklets.

    Booking order

    Top first: what would force a date change, or can't be worked around
    1. RealNZ Wanderer, Tue 5 Oct, twin-share, room 11 or 15. Bookable now; the trip is built around this sailing. Full refund if cancelled more than 14 days out.
    2. Brookby Lodge, 29 Sep to 11 Oct. Small kennel, school holidays, no easy fallback.
    3. Northern Explorer, Thu 30 Sep and Sun 10 Oct. Three days a week each way: if full, the trip shifts. Book the day 2027 opens.
    4. Coastal Pacific, Fri 1 and Sat 9 Oct. Fallback: drive Picton–Christchurch. Book with the Northern Explorer.
    5. Interislander on the connecting sailings, 1 and 9 Oct. Book with the trains.
    6. The Hermitage, 2 Oct (and 3 Oct if kept). Decide the second night first.
    7. Te Anau, Mon 4 Oct, the night before the cruise.
    8. Then the easy ones: rental car in Christchurch, Wellington and Christchurch nights, Wanaka or Hawea, Finn for the dogs.
