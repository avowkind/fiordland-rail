Waiheke to the fiords, by train

A once-in-a-lifetime journey down the length of New Zealand: rail through both islands, then Fiordland by boat. We want unhurried time together, everything handled, a real sense of seeing the whole country, wildlife and wild places, and some memorable food and wine, without being stressed by long drives, tight connections or early starts.

What this site does

It pulls together what we've learned about the trains, the ferry and the Fiordland cruises, so we can see which dates line up, what each option feels like, and what our chosen trip would cost and take. Tick options in Discovery and the plan, map, budget and night count update as you go.

Our ground rules

  • Two weeks ideally, three at most, because of Poppy and Rua
  • Shoulder season: spring 2027 or autumn 2028; winter allowed
  • Twin beds at minimum, separate rooms where it's worth it
  • Premium where it matters, sensible elsewhere

When do the trains and the boats line up?

Rail, ferry and Fiordland cruise availability from October 2026 to June 2028, alongside school holidays, tourist peaks and sandfly season. Solid bars are published dates. Hatched bars are projected from past patterns or not yet confirmed.

Winter cruises, no Coastal Pacific

Most small-ship departures fall between May and early September, when the Picton–Christchurch train doesn't run.

Late September is the pinch point

Heritage Explorer's 19 Sep voyage needs the Coastal Pacific running by about 16 Sep. Its 2027 start date is unconfirmed, and the 25 Sep sailing overlaps the school holidays.

October and April suit RealNZ

Two-night cruises on the Wanderer (shared bathrooms) sit inside both preferred windows, with all three trains running.

    4 · Things to think about

    Not costed yet
    • Where the car stays while we're away (2–3 weeks). Options: leave it at home on Waiheke if a dog taxi works; long-stay parking at Half Moon Bay; Auckland Airport long-term parking, which suits flying home because you land beside the car and Brookby is about 25 minutes away; ask Brookby whether a car can stay on site; or a friend or family driveway. The house-sitters could also take it back to the island.
    • Self-drive plus rail home. The rental goes back to Christchurch: Manapouri to Christchurch is about 7.5–8 hours, split via Wanaka and Tekapo. Stay in Christchurch before the 07:00 Coastal Pacific. You could save that night by dropping the car near Addington station before 6am, but only if a depot there has an after-hours key drop. Most depots are at the airport.
    • Night counts are approximate. An extra Wellington or Picton night, or a Fiordland weather buffer day, adds to both the total and the kennel bill.
    • Kennel cough booster must be current for the whole stay. Check the vaccination booklets.
    • Gold Card discount: 10% on Great Journeys fares for holders. Check whether it applies to both of us.
    • Travel insurance that covers weather cancellations in Fiordland.
