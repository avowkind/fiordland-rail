    Baggage limits

    Per person; the tightest leg sets the limit
    • Trains, Scenic Plus (GJNZ136974): two checked bags of up to 23 kg and 158 cm (height + length + width) each, plus one cabin bag up to 7 kg and 118 cm. On board you can keep a small day pack without wheels, a handbag, camera and coat. Wheeled or hard cabin bags go in the baggage van. Extra bag $35; repack bag $20. Nothing over 23 kg is accepted.
    • Interislander (71011026, foot passengers): two checked bags of up to 23 kg and 200 cm each, plus one small bag and a handbag or laptop bag on board. Wheeled cabin bags and backpacks must be checked in. Extra bags $20 each. On 1 Oct tell Wellington check-in we connect to the Coastal Pacific and they transfer the bags; we still check in at the Great Journeys desk in the Picton terminal.
    • RealNZ Wanderer, 5–7 Oct: an overnight bag only on board. The main luggage stays in the rental car on View Street, Manapouri.
    • Rental car, 2–8 Oct: no limit, but the boot has to take everything for the South Island loop.
    • In practice: one 23 kg suitcase each plus a small day pack (no wheels) fits every leg; the Wanderer overnight bag can be a soft bag packed inside.
